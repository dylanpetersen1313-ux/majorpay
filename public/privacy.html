Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Short version: this site doesn't collect your data

  • MajorPay has no accounts, no sign-up, no forms, and sets no cookies or trackers of its own.
  • We do not collect, store, or sell any personal information about visitors.
  • The search tools on this site run entirely in your browser against a public dataset. Nothing you type is sent to a server or logged anywhere.

Hosting logs

This site is hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel's infrastructure may process standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) to serve pages and keep the service secure. MajorPay's operator does not have access to or use this data. See Vercel's privacy policy for details.

Third-party data

The salary and college cost figures shown come from public government datasets (see the Terms page for details). Viewing that data does not involve sending any of your information to the U.S. Department of Education, NCES, or any college.

Children's privacy

This site does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13. Since no personal information is collected at all, there is nothing to gather from any visitor of any age.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the site evolves; the date above reflects the latest revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the site's operator.
