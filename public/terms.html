2. Where the data comes from

Earnings and enrollment figures are sourced from the U.S. Department of Education's College Scorecard, a public dataset. Figures shown are median earnings of bachelor's degree graduates measured 1–4 years after graduation, as published by the Department of Education.

Tuition and net-price figures are derived from IPEDS (the Integrated Postsecondary Education Data System), a service of the National Center for Education Statistics. Sticker prices for the most recent academic years are projected forward from historical IPEDS data using each institution's own compound annual growth rate, not directly reported figures. Treat recent-year prices as estimates. Average net price by income bracket is calculated by applying each institution's most recently reported discount rate.

We do not independently verify this data and are not responsible for errors, omissions, or delays in the source data.
